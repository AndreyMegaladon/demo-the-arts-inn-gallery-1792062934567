La Nostra Storia

The Arts Inn is a unique and intimate art gallery and cocktail bar located in the heart of Florence, nestled in a historic 15th-century building just steps from Santa Maria Novella. Once a renowned framer's workshop for fifty years, it later became the painting atelier of an artist who envisioned this wonderful space combining visual art with a cozy lounge atmosphere. The gallery showcases a curated blend of classic and contemporary realism, as well as works celebrating craftsmanship, sculpture, and photography, providing both a physical and virtual platform for talented emerging and established artists.

More like a welcoming lounge than a conventional gallery, The Arts Inn invites visitors to admire and purchase original artwork while sipping fine local wine or expertly crafted, artisanal cocktails. The venue is characterized by its boho charm, quirky artworks, mismatched furnishings, and a carefully curated soundtrack featuring rock, jazz, blues, ambient, and trip-hop music. It serves as a vibrant cultural hub where creativity, great drinks, and exceptional company seamlessly merge for an unforgettable Florentine experience.
